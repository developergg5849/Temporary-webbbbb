Learning path

From player to creator in three steps

1. Play with purpose

Analyse why a game works: its loop, its pacing, its feel.

2. Build small

Make tiny games quickly and finish them. Every finished project teaches more than ten abandoned ones.

3. Ship and grow

Publish, gather feedback, then join a studio or start your own.
